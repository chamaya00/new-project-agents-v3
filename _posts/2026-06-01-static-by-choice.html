--- layout: entry title: "Static by choice" summary: "No JavaScript, no framework, no package manager - the constraints are the design, not a phase before the real site." ---

People keep asking when this site is getting a "real" front end. The honest answer is that it already has one - it is just not the one they mean. Every page here is HTML5 and CSS3, hand-authored, and nothing else runs in the browser. There is no build step to break, no dependency to patch on a Friday, and no bundle size to watch. That is not nostalgia; it is a bet that a personal site with a few dozen pages does not need the tooling a product does.

The one concession is Jekyll, and it runs entirely on GitHub's own infrastructure at push time - nothing I install or maintain locally changes because of it. It turns two folders of hand-authored files into listing pages, and that is the whole job I asked it to do. Everything else - the layout, the copy, the way a page links to another - is still just markup I wrote myself.
