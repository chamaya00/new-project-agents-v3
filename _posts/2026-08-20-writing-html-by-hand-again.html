--- title: "Writing HTML by hand again" summary: "A few years of JSX later, closing my own tags feels like relearning to drive a stick shift." ---

Every element on this site is something I typed myself, closing tag and all, and it took longer than I expected to stop reaching for a component library that isn't there. The muscle memory from years of JSX doesn't disappear just because the project doesn't need it anymore.

What's surprised me is how little I miss it for a site this size. A handful of pages, a shared nav, one stylesheet - the overhead a framework removes was never large enough here to be worth the overhead it adds. Reading the finished markup start to finish, with nothing generated in between, has been the closest thing to relearning what the browser actually does with the tags I hand it.
