--- layout: entry title: "A week with Jekyll" ---

I spent most of this week wiring up Jekyll's collections rather than writing anything I'd call content, which is a strange way to spend a week on a blog. The payoff is that projects and posts are now folders of files instead of pages I hand-edited every time something new shipped - add a file, get a listing entry and a page for it, in that order, with no template copy-pasted from the last one.

The part that took longest wasn't the collections themselves, it was making the build fail loudly when a file's front matter is broken instead of quietly dropping the file from every listing. A build that goes green on a typo is worse than one that never ran, because nothing tells you to go looking for what's missing.

Next up is making the two collections talk to each other - shared tags, a page per tag - but that is next week's problem, not this one's.
